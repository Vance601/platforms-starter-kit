# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026  
**Last Revised:** November 23, 2026

Dugger's Holding Company LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company, doing business as Battery City (“Battery City,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), respects your privacy. This Privacy Notice describes the types of information we may collect from you when you visit [https://www.battery-city.com](https://www.battery-city.com), [https://www.battery.city](https://www.battery.city), or use the Battery City web application, including staff and driver pages used in a mobile browser (collectively, the “Services”). It also describes how we use the information we collect, with whom we share it, how we protect it, and the choices we offer you. If you do not agree with this Privacy Notice, please do not access or use any part of the Services.

Battery City is a software platform for battery shops, warehouses, and fleets to manage inventory, sales, warranties, drivers, and core returns. The legal entity that operates the Services is Dugger's Holding Company LLC, with a place of business at 4202 E Elwood St Suite 31, Phoenix, AZ 85040.

**1. Information We Collect and How We Collect It**

**Information you provide.** We collect information you or a business account submit to the Services, including:

- Account and business information when you sign up or are added as a user: your name, work email address, a password (stored in hashed form), company or organization name, location name, city, state, time zone, and business settings such as a barcode prefix or a default core-charge amount.
- Driver records created by a business account, which may include a driver's name, phone number, and a personal identification number (PIN) stored in hashed form.
- If you choose to sign in with GitHub, we receive the identifier and profile information GitHub provides to complete that sign-in.
- When you request a demo, we collect your name, email address, company name, and, if you choose to provide them, your phone number and website, along with the referring website and any marketing campaign parameters from your visit.
- When a seller records a sale or installation, the Services may store the customer's name and phone number, email address if provided, vehicle identification number (VIN), vehicle description (such as year, make, model, and engine), call or job numbers, warranty details, and core-return information.
- Photos that you or a seller upload to help read a VIN.
- Messages you send us through a form on the website or by mail.

**Information collected automatically.** When you use the Services, we may collect:

- Your Internet protocol (IP) address, browser type, the date and time of requests, and similar technical log data collected in the ordinary course of operating and securing the Services. We also record the IP address when you start a sign-up.
- Session cookies needed to keep you signed in, including cookies named manager_session and driver_session, and authentication cookies used for sign-in. These cookies are required for the Services to function for signed-in users.
- When you visit our marketing pages, we may store marketing campaign parameters (such as utm_source) and the referring website in your browser's session storage, and include them if you later submit the demo inquiry form.

**Public warranty lookup.** If you use our warranty lookup page, you provide a phone number or VIN, and we use that information to find matching warranty records a seller has entered in the Services.

**Information we do not collect in the Services today.** We do not collect precise geolocation (GPS) from your device. We do not collect battery charge level, health, usage, or diagnostic telemetry. Inventory tracking records a battery's business status and assigned warehouse, truck, or sale record — not its physical GPS location or electrical condition. We do not use advertising or remarketing cookies or pixels in the Services today. We do not collect or store full payment card numbers for battery purchases; sellers handle payment for those sales outside the Services.

**2. Cookies and Similar Technologies; “Do Not Track” Signals**

We use cookies and similar storage as described above to operate the Services and keep you signed in. You can often control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking session cookies will prevent you from staying signed in.

A do not track signal (“DNT”) is a browser setting intended to limit tracking across websites. You can learn more about DNT at [https://allaboutdnt.com](https://allaboutdnt.com). At this time, the Services do not respond to DNT signals or Global Privacy Control signals, and the Services may continue to collect information in the manner described in this Privacy Notice.

**3. How We Use the Information We Collect**

We may use the information we collect to:

- provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Services;
- create and administer accounts, authenticate users and drivers, and secure the Services;
- record inventory, transfers, sales, warranties, and core returns for the business that uses the Services;
- provide public warranty lookup when a visitor submits a VIN or phone number;
- respond to demo requests and account sign-up, including sending verification and password-reset emails;
- communicate with you about your account or the Services;
- detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and
- comply with law and enforce our terms.

If we send promotional email, we will include a way to unsubscribe. You may still receive non-promotional messages about your account or the Services.

**4. Disclosure of Information**

We may share information as follows:

- with service providers that host and operate the Services, including our website host and database provider, and our transactional email provider;
- with the seller or company whose account holds the data, including that business's staff and drivers, who can see customer, sale, and inventory information for that business;
- with professional advisers, or when we believe disclosure is required or appropriate to comply with law, a legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Battery City, our users, or others;
- with a buyer or successor if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets; and
- with your consent or at your direction.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

**5. Data Security**

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards we consider appropriate, including transmitting the Services over HTTPS and storing passwords and driver PINs in hashed form. The electronic transmission of information is not completely secure. We cannot guarantee that the safeguards we have in place will never be defeated or fail, or that they will always be sufficient or effective. Any transmission of personal information is at your own risk.

**6. Promotional Communications**

If you have received a promotional email from us, you can opt out of receiving future promotional emails from us by following the unsubscribe instructions contained in such email. You may still receive non-promotional messages about your account or the Services.

**7. Accessing, Correcting, and Deleting Your Information**

You can review and change certain personal information associated with a signed-in account in the account areas of the Services. There is currently no self-service account-deletion control in the Services. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, write to us at the mailing address in Section 12. We may not accommodate a request if we believe the change would violate any law or legal requirement, cause the information to be incorrect, or if we must or may retain the information to complete transactions, honor warranty or core-return records, resolve disputes, or meet other legal or legitimate business purposes. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

**8. No Underage Users**

The Services are not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from, anyone under the age of 18. If we learn we have collected or received personal information from anyone under the age of 18, we will delete that information. If you believe we might have any information from or about anyone under the age of 18, please write to us at the address in Section 12.

**9. Retention of Your Information**

We will keep your information while you have an account with us or for as long as necessary in connection with our provision of the Services, including inventory, warranty, and core-return records. Thereafter, we will keep your information only as long as necessary to keep records required by law or as needed for legitimate business purposes such as dispute resolution, tax and accounting, and fraud prevention. We do not publish a fixed deletion schedule.

**10. Changes to Our Privacy Notice**

We reserve the right to update or change this Privacy Notice at any time. If we make a material change, we will provide notice (for example, by posting on our website) and will provide a link to the new policy. In the event we make a material change to how we use your personal information, we will provide you with an opportunity to opt out of such new or different use. The date this Privacy Notice was last revised is at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Notice periodically.

We are based in Arizona in the United States. When we obtain information about you, we may transfer, process, and store such information in the United States or in other countries where our service providers operate. Those places may have different data-protection laws than the country where you reside. By using the Services, you consent to that transfer, processing, and storage.
