# About Us

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

Battery City is a software platform that helps battery shops, warehouses, and mobile or roadside service fleets track inventory, record sales and installations, manage drivers and trucks, handle core returns, and look up warranties. Sellers can scan a battery and a vehicle identification number (VIN) at the roadside or in the shop, keep warehouse-to-truck-to-customer-to-core records, and let customers look up a warranty by VIN or phone.

The platform is operated by Dugger's Holding Company LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company (New Mexico Secretary of State Control Number 7639651), doing business as Battery City. Our websites are [https://www.battery-city.com](https://www.battery-city.com) and [https://www.battery.city](https://www.battery.city). The Services include these websites and the Battery City web application, including staff and driver pages that can be used in a mobile browser.

Battery City provides the software. Each seller or company that uses Battery City sets its own prices, payment methods, delivery or service area, fees, return and cancellation terms, core charges, core refunds, and core-return terms. A battery purchase is a transaction between the customer and that seller.
