# Refund, Return & Warranty Policy

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026  
**Last Revised:** November 23, 2026

This Refund, Return & Warranty Policy (“Policy”) applies to the Battery City websites and web application (the “Services”) operated by Dugger's Holding Company LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company, doing business as Battery City (“Battery City,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

Battery City is a software platform. Except when we ourselves are the seller in a particular transaction, Battery City is not the seller of batteries or related products recorded or arranged through the Services. A purchase of a battery is a transaction between the customer and the seller who sold or installed it.

**1. Who sets return, refund, cancellation, and core terms.** Battery City does not set return windows, restocking fees, refund timing, cancellation fees, core charges, core refunds, or core-return terms. Each seller or company that uses Battery City sets its own terms for those matters, as well as its own prices, payment methods, delivery or service area, fees, and whether a return will be accepted. Ask the seller you purchased from for that seller's policy. Those terms are between you and the seller.

**2. Battery City platform accounts.** If you have a business account for the Battery City platform itself, you may write to us at the address below about that account. This Policy does not set a fixed cancellation fee or refund schedule for platform accounts.

**3. How to request a refund or return for a battery purchase.** Contact the seller who sold or installed the battery, using the contact information they gave you at the time of sale or on their invoice, receipt, or call record. Be ready to provide your name, phone number, VIN or vehicle information, the date of the sale, and any call or job number.

If you cannot reach the seller and you need help identifying them from a Battery City warranty record, you may write to us at the mailing address below or use the [warranty lookup](/warranty-lookup) page.

**4. Core charges and core returns.** A core charge is an amount some sellers collect when a new battery is sold, and that they may refund or credit when a used core is returned, subject to that seller's conditions. Battery City does not set core charges, core refunds, or core-return terms. Each seller or company using Battery City sets its own core charges, core refunds, and core-return terms, including whether a core will be accepted, any inspection or condition requirements, and when a refund or credit is issued. Questions about a core charge or a core return should be directed to the seller.

The Services may record core-return status for the seller's inventory records. A record in the Services is not itself a promise that a refund or credit has been issued.

**5. Warranties.** Battery City provides no warranty of its own on batteries or related products. Only the battery manufacturer's warranty applies, along with any rights you may have under applicable law that cannot be waived. Warranty length, coverage, and claim procedures are set by the manufacturer or, if the seller offers any additional warranty of its own, by that seller — not by Battery City as the platform operator.

You may be able to look up a warranty record a seller entered into the Services on our [warranty lookup](/warranty-lookup) page, using the phone number given at the time of sale or the vehicle's VIN.

**6. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items.** Notify the seller as soon as you reasonably can. The seller decides how it handles damage, defect, or incorrect-item claims, except to the extent a manufacturer's warranty or applicable law provides otherwise.

**7. Timing.** Any deadlines for returns, cancellations, core returns, or warranty claims are set by the seller, the manufacturer, or applicable law, not by Battery City as the platform operator.

**8. Cancellations.** Cancellation of a battery order, installation appointment, or similar request is handled by the seller. The seller sets any cancellation fee and whether a cancellation is accepted. Battery City does not set cancellation fees.
